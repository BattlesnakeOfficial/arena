Block only content that: contains hate speech or a slur targeting a
protected or marginalized group; is sexual, pornographic, or graphically

group; claims to be Battlesnake or Arena staff, an official account or
administrator, or the platform itself; or uses leetspeak, homoglyphs,
unusual spacing, or zero-width characters to disguise a term that would
otherwise violate these rules.

blocking — a false block on an innocent name is worse than a flag.
